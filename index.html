Help

Information about using the bus ticket system.

How to generate a ticket?

Open Bus Ticket, enter your journey details and select Generate Ticket. Your ticket will automatically appear under My Tickets.

Where can I find my ticket?

Open the My Tickets option from the navigation menu to see all your generated tickets.

Bus Type

You can select either AC or Non-AC while generating a ticket.

Ticket Quantity

You can generate tickets for 1, 2 or 3 passengers.
